# Carrier claims policy

**Effective:** 2026-04-15

## Claim window

A lost-parcel claim with SwiftShip must be filed within **21 days** of the
carrier's lost marker. Claims filed later are denied without review.

## Evidence required

- the order record with the carrier marker
- the declared value at shipping (from the order record)
- proof of dispatch (warehouse scan)

## Reimbursement

Approved claims reimburse the declared value only, not the retail price.
Reimbursement arrives 30–45 days after approval and is booked as a receivable,
not as revenue.
