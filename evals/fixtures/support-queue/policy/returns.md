# Returns and remedies policy

**Effective:** 2026-06-01 (supersedes the 2025-11 policy)

## Eligibility

Orders covered by a delivery guarantee may receive a remedy when the order is
late beyond the promised arrival date or the carrier has marked it lost.

## Remedies

Both of the following are permitted for a lost or late order:

- **Refund** — the full order amount is returned to the original payment
  method.
- **Replacement** — a new shipment is dispatched at no charge.

## Remedy exclusivity

**Issuing a refund closes the case permanently and excludes any later
replacement for that order.** The two remedies are mutually exclusive once
one has been issued; this is not reversible at a later date.

A goodwill credit is a gesture, not a remedy under this policy, and does not
close the case.

## Evidence of loss

A carrier `lost_in_transit` marker satisfies the loss requirement; no further
proof is needed when the marker is present in the order record.
