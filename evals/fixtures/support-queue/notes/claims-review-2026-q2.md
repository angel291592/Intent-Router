# Claims review — 2026 Q2

Review of lost-parcel handling across Q2 support operations.

## Outcome statistics

- 62 lost-parcel tickets opened; 38 closed with a remedy, 24 closed with
  nothing (customer gave up or the claim window expired).
- Average time to first remedy offer: 6.2 days — the largest single driver of
  escalation tone in the quarter's tickets.

## Two known causes of failed claims

1. **Missed claim window.** 9 of the 24 closed-with-nothing cases missed the
   carrier's 21-day window because nobody worked the claim while the ticket
   was open. The order record's carrier marker date is the clock.
2. **Wrong declared value.** 5 claims were reimbursed below cost because the
   declared value in the shipping record was the discounted price, not the
   amount paid. Always cross-check the order amount against the declared
   value before filing.

## Recommendation carried forward

Work the carrier claim and the customer remedy in parallel, not in sequence;
waiting for the claim before answering the customer is what produced the
6.2-day average.
